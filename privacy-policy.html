Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lasag Dev ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile applications and video games distributed through the Amazon Appstore—including our titles Stickman Ping Pong Table, Bamboo Skyguard, Stretchy Hero Bullet Escape, and Mob Shove Crowd Clash—and when you visit our website lasagdev.com (the "Site").

By downloading, installing, or playing our Apps, or using our Site, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not download or use our Apps.

Privacy Commitment: In our titles (Stickman Ping Pong Table, Bamboo Skyguard, Stretchy Hero Bullet Escape, and Mob Shove Crowd Clash), we do NOT require user account creation, we do NOT collect your contact address book, photos, microphone or camera data, and we do NOT sell your personal information. Our games are built solely for fun, lightweight mobile entertainment.

1. Who We Are

Lasag Dev is an independent digital game and software development studio operating under the developer name Lasag Dev on the Amazon Appstore and via lasagdev.com. For any inquiries regarding your privacy or data rights, please contact us at support@lasagdev.com.

2. Games Covered by This Policy

This Privacy Policy specifically applies to all games and applications published by Lasag Dev on the Amazon Appstore, including:

  • Stickman Ping Pong Table
  • Bamboo Skyguard
  • Stretchy Hero Bullet Escape
  • Mob Shove Crowd Clash

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

We do not require you to register an account or enter a password to play our games. The only personal information we collect directly is what you provide when writing to our customer support (such as your email address and any bug reports or feedback sent to support@lasagdev.com).

3.2 Technical & Device Information Collected Automatically

When you play our Apps on Amazon Fire OS or compatible Android-based devices, technical diagnostic information may be collected automatically by your device's operating system or embedded SDKs:

  • Device & System Specifications: Device model (e.g. Amazon Fire HD tablet, Fire TV), operating system version, system language, screen resolution, and available memory.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Standard resettable advertising IDs (such as the Amazon Advertising ID) used to deliver non-intrusive or personalized ads where permitted.
  • Gameplay Telemetry & Diagnostics: Session timestamps, levels reached, high scores, crash stack traces, and performance stability metrics to help us fix glitches.
  • Coarse Location (Country Level): Derived from IP address to ensure regional compliance (e.g. EU GDPR consent prompts). We never collect or track precise GPS location.

3.3 Website Information (lasagdev.com)

The website lasagdev.com is a static informational studio showcase. We do not use third-party marketing tracking cookies or profiling pixels. Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, request time) are kept temporarily by our hosting provider for cybersecurity and DDoS mitigation.

4. How We Use Collected Data

We process information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and deliver gameplay in Stickman Ping Pong Table, Bamboo Skyguard, Stretchy Hero Bullet Escape, and Mob Shove Crowd Clash.
  • To display in-game ads that keep our games free to download.
  • To identify technical errors, fix app crashes, and optimize battery performance.
  • To reply to your player support inquiries via support@lasagdev.com.
  • To comply with Amazon Appstore developer distribution policies and international data laws.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our games may incorporate software development kits (SDKs) from trusted third-party providers who act as independent controllers under their respective privacy policies:

  • Amazon Mobile Ads & Appstore Services: Amazon Privacy Notice
  • Unity Ads & Gaming Services: Unity Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for commercial outbound marketing.

6. Advertising Choices & Opt-Out

You can manage and opt out of interest-based personalized advertising on your Amazon or Android-based devices at any time:

  • Amazon Fire OS / Fire Tablets: Go to Settings → Security & Privacy → Advertising ID → toggle off "Interest-Based Ads".
  • Android Devices: Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → tap "Delete advertising ID" or opt out of personalized ads.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our games are built for broad audience entertainment and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has submitted personal details through customer support correspondence, please contact us at support@lasagdev.com and we will delete it immediately.

8. Data Retention & Security

Technical diagnostics and aggregated gameplay metrics are kept only as long as necessary for game balance and maintenance (typically up to 14 months), after which data is purged. Player support emails are retained strictly for the duration needed to resolve the inquiry.

We employ standard TLS/HTTPS transport encryption to secure data transmission between devices and servers.

9. Player Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of whether you are located in the European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or California, we respect your statutory rights to access, rectify, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California consumer privacy laws.

To exercise your rights, email us at support@lasagdev.com. We respond to all requests within 30 days without fee.
